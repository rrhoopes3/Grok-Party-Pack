# The Aftermath — The Official Tavern of the Relic Civilization

A self-contained, rowdy bar where the gods, presidents, and relic personifications drink, gossip, fight, and turn the day's chaos into even more lore.

## Run

```bash
python relic-tavern/web_app.py
```

http://localhost:5012

Hit "OPEN THE TAVERN" and watch the gods and presidents get drunk and tell increasingly unhinged stories about what just happened in the other relics.

## What It Does

- Pulls recent drama from the League, Gazette, Broadcasts, etc.
- Generates a full night of tavern log with multiple named patrons
- Features real grudges, headlines, and events turned into bar gossip
- Archives every night in `~/.relic-tavern/nights.json`

## Why This Is Peak Chaos

The relics have been generating incredible drama all day.

They now have a place to *process* that drama while getting absolutely hammered.

Future loops that create more suffering will automatically produce better, funnier, more unhinged tavern nights. The bar is self-sustaining.

This is the "the relics have a social life" moment.
