## What It Does

- Pulls real recent drama from the League, Gazette, Tavern, Broadcasts, Post Office, Bulletin Board, etc.
- Generates tarot-style readings with relic-flavored cards
- Written in the exact dramatic voices of the Pantheon and Presidential Council
- Archives every reading in `~/.relic-tarot/readings.json`
