# Relic Radio — 96.6 THE FORGE

The official late-night broadcast network of the Grok Party Pack.

It turns the output of the other relics into dramatic radio episodes, call-in shows, and breaking news segments.

## Run

```bash
python relic-radio/web_app.py
```

http://localhost:5017

Big beautiful fake radio player. Hit "PLAY RANDOM EPISODE" or "REQUEST A DISPATCH" on any topic you want the gods and presidents to weigh in on.

## What It Does

- Steals recent sagas, grudges, and broadcasts from the other relics
- Generates gloriously over-the-top radio episodes in mixed god + president voices
- Lets you request custom dispatches ("the current state of my token usage", "why my last arena match went so poorly", etc.)
- Looks like the most cursed pirate radio station in the 24th century

## Why This Is Excellent Chaos

The relics were already talking *about* each other in their own little corners.

Now they have a shared radio station.

Future loops that generate more content in the Chronicler, League, Broadcast, or Museum will automatically leak into the radio rotation. The signal is self-reinforcing.

This is the "the relics have formed a media empire" moment.

## Related Relics (the ones currently on the air)

- `chronicler/` — primary source of the best sagas
- `pantheon-league/` — primary source of the best grudges and drama
- `chaos-broadcast/` — emergency transmissions that sometimes interrupt regular programming
- `relic-museum/` — the institution whose exhibits occasionally get discussed on air
- `relic-oracle/` (port 5008) — the Council sometimes broadcasts its latest prophecies here first.
